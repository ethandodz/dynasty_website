Week 1 of the first season in Ball Knowers history. Startup draft hangovers were cured, a 200-point game happened, and at least three managers learned the hard way that the bench doesn't count.

## WoahItsKyle vs BangUpBobby | Kyle Drops 201 and Sends Everyone Else Home Early

Everybody showed up to Week 1 with startup draft optimism. Brian left with a 100-point loss. Kyle's Old Heads hung 201.46 on the board, the highest score of the week by 55 points and a number that's going to be hanging over this league for a while.

Everybody started cooking: Josh Allen (39.7), Derrick Henry (35.3), Kenneth Walker (34.1) and Chris Olave (28.2). Dalton Kincaid even chipped in 18. And the scary part? Kyle left another 38 points on the bench, including Bryce Young (37.4) and Jalen Coker (33.8). A perfect lineup would have scored 239. Disgusting.

Brian actually scored 100.96, which beats 6 other teams this week, but it doesn't matter when you draw the guy who put up a video game score. Puka (12.4) was quiet, and the WR3/kicker combo of Waddle (1.2), Shaheed (1.4) and Mevis (1.0) combined for 3.6 points. Waddle's 1.2 on Monday night was officially the difference between losing by 100 and losing by double. Moral victories.

Kyle starts 1-0 at the top of the league. Brian starts 0-1 wondering who he angered.

## lorc vs TheBastardInTheNorth | Swift, Goedert Power Lorc to a Statement Win

Lorcan put up 146.36, the second-highest score of the week, and did it the right way: he only left 0.7 points on his bench. That's a perfect week of lineup setting, which is more than most of this league can say.

D'Andre Swift led the way with 32.4, and Dallas Goedert (23.7) is already making that 10th-round pick look like theft. Breece Hall added 19.8. CeeDee Lamb (15.4) and Herbert (15.3) were just fine, and they didn't need to be more than that.

Jaime's team had a rough opener: 84.34 points, 13th of 16 teams. Jared Goff (20.4) and Chase Brown (18.8) did their part, but Malik Nabers had 12.9, Brian Thomas Jr. had 7, and Calvin Ridley put up a big fat zero in the starting lineup. Even with a perfect lineup this one was a 92-point performance, so there's no bench to blame here. Just a bad week.

Lorc sits at 1-0 and second in the league on points. The North has some work to do.

## bradleyweik vs beniquez | Bradleyweik Opens With 143 While Garry Benches the Wrong QB

This matchup was decided on the bench, and not in a good way for Garry. He started Bo Nix (7.4) over Brock Purdy (27.1). That's a 20-point swing on one decision, and Los Conejos Malos lost by 36, so it didn't single-handedly cost him the game. But a perfect lineup would have scored 142.10, which is basically what bradleyweik scored. Garry left 35 points on the pine. Just saying.

Bradleyweik went 143.10, third-best in the league, behind Trevor Lawrence (34.1), Ashton Jeanty (32.7) and Zay Flowers (26.0). The tight end slot gave him 3.8 points from Greg Dulcich and it didn't matter at all. This owner is built different.

Jahmyr Gibbs (33.6) did what Gibbs does, and Ladd McConkey (19.2) was solid. Then Tony Pollard had 4.4 and Jake Ferguson 2.6. Garry's 106.94 would've beaten 9 teams this week. He just drew the wrong one.

Bradleyweik starts 1-0 and leads the South. Garry starts 0-1 and has some lineup soul-searching to do.

## Squallie vs juebelacker | Caleb Williams Goes Off, Burrow-Chase Stack Collapses

Walter's three-headed RB monster showed up exactly as advertised. Jonathan Taylor (25.1), Javonte Williams (24.2) and Kyle Monangai (20.4) combined for nearly 70 points. Caleb Williams added 41.3, the best QB score of the week. Squallie put up 141.56, fourth-best in the league.

On the other side, Justin's whole strategy is the Burrow-to-Chase stack, and in Week 1 that stack was Burrow 16.2, Chase 3.2. Rough. Davante Adams had 5.6 and Kenny Gainwell had 2.8 at RB2. Meanwhile Jordan Love sat on Justin's bench with 23.5 and Jacoby Brissett had 18.5. Justin left 25 points on the bench and still would have lost by 34, so the problem wasn't the lineup. It was the stack.

The 59-point margin was the third-biggest blowout of the week. Squallie starts 1-0, Justin starts 0-1 with 82.26 points, 14th of 16.

## dodz vs muebe5 | Commish Survives Darnold's Exit (and His Own Lineup)

Team Dodz wins by 18 and goes 1-0, which is the only thing that saves the commish from a week of group chat abuse. Throwsa Parks starts 0-1, but anyone who drafted a roster full of rookies probably expected that.
