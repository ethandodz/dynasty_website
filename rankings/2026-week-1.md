Ball Knowers Power Rankings

Week 1 Power Rankings

1. WoahItsKyle [Kyle] (1-0). Old head team showed out in week 1. Looks like Josh Allen is ready for another big year. 201 points will stand as a high score for a long time I think. I have nothing bad to say. Really just an all-time great week, actually I have one bad thing to say: it’s all down hill from here.
2. Team Lorc [Lorcan] (1-0). Great performance with 146 points, Hall and Swift seem like a nice dynamic duo grabbed in rounds 3&5. WRs need to live up to their draft position. Goedert also seeming like a 10th round steal.
3. BradleyWeik (1-0). Flowers & Lawrence looking like bargains, while Jeanty is starting his breakout sophomore campaign. No tight end no problem for this team. Did we expect anything different? This owner is too good.
4. Squallie [Walter] (1-0). JT, Javonte, & Monangai is quite the 3-headed monster at RB, a luxury most of us could only dream of. Receivers leave a little to be desired, though. Travis Hunter might be an an all time fumble but Caleb Williams is becoming generational.
5. Merc & Friends [Logan] (1-0). This was the last team to pick a QB and it shows. 6 QBs on this roster combining for 25 pts. Luckily Bucky, Amon Ra, & Likely came to play. Caleb Douglas is begging for playing time on the bench. This team could be a dark horse if Likely has a top 3 year, although I’m not sure how Likely that is.
6. Love Hurts [Maurice] (1-0). Hurts & Bijan prove they’re a dynamic duo & Evans showing that he’s aging like a fine wine. Saquon needs to prove he’s not a one hit wonder on the Eagles while Harrison Jr. is proving why he’s a bust & Aiyuk is wondering why he was drafted in the 8th round. The bench on this team is a problem.
7. Boricua Bandits [Wilberto] (1-0). Imagine getting carried by David Montgomery in 2026. AJ Brown heads to IR but it doesn’t matter because this team has old heads for days that can fill in from the bench. Would’ve lost in a harder matchup but that’s why we play. the. games.
8. Team Dodz [Ethan] (1-0). Darnold goes out on the first drive but luckily I was playing Throwsa Parks. Jjettas put on a clinic in week 1 matching his TD total from last season & Nico Collins joined in the fun as well. Those 2 will have to carry this team. Would’ve been a respectable score with Mahomes in the lineup but I was playing with my food. RB on this team also scares me.
9. Los Conejos Malos [Garry] (0-1). Might want to Nix Bo from the starting lineup after that performance, not that it would’ve mattered vs bradleyweik. Hopefully Gibbs doesn’t get overworked from supporting the Lions & this fantasy team. Ladd taking a hospital ball from Herbert might hurt the chance of that. Regardless, this team does have nice pieces to build around like Tate who can hopefully leave the titans ASAP.
10. Team BangUpBobby [Brian] (0-1). Waddle with a Monday night miracle putting up 1.2 to save Brian from getting doubled up by Kyle. Puka is going to need to be better if this team wants to reach the next level. Solid performers on the bench though this team is built for the long haul. Two Jets in the starting lineup up though, that might be a crime.
11. Team marsolo [Marshall] (0-1). Shoutout to Seven Oaks Elementary & JLMS alum Christian Watson, he tried his best but wasn’t able to overcome the Bower outage. Combined 9.9 points from your first two picks is hard to overcome. Having Lamar is always fun though and he seems like he’s due for a solid year. An easy matchup next week should get this team back on track.
12. Team juebelacker [Justin] (0-1). I don’t think this team is bad but they had a very bad week. We all knew this team would live & die by the Burrow/Chase stack; this week they died. Gainwell as RB2 hurts but hopefully Burrow, Chase, & Adams can be more reliable most weeks. Maybe B Robinson from the falcons can play like the other B Robinson from the falcons.
13. Team carbyy [David] (0-1). This team was a victim to offseason propaganda as evidenced by drafting Parker Washington, Stribling, Cyrus Allen, & Q Johnston. Maybe he should’ve taken a propa ganda at the depth chart to see Cyrus Allen is WR5. However, it looks like McBride and Washington are good cornerstones to build around, even if Washington is a little zesty.
14. Team rtuebe [Ryan] (0-1). Maye, CMC, & JSN seems like a reliable trio to build around in rounds 1-3 but they need to play better if they’re gonna make up for Harold Fannin who was picked in the 4th round. This team needs Josh Jacobs to get out of prison now. 
15. TheBastardInTheNorth [Jaime] (0-1). This team has some fine pieces with Brown, Nabers, & Warren but unfortunately had a bad showing in week 1. I have faith this team can bounce back after a poor week 1 performance. I think off the field issues were affecting the way this team played, but with those issues now resolved look for this team to rally.
16. Throwsa Parks [Mark] (0-1). Most of these players were playing in their first career nfl game & it showed. I see the potential with Dart & Love but only time will tell if the rest of the young guys can become reliable players. I have a feeling this is where Mark expected to be after week 1.
