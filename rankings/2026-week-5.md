Week 5 Power Rankings

1. BangUpBobby ⬆️9️⃣ (4-1). Puka is balling. Kyren is balling. G Wilson & stroud are playing well. What seemed like a mid team after week 1 is now on a 4 game win streak with the 2nd most points in the league. Many solid contributors on the bench ready to step in, too. The only weakness on this team is the chubby kicker who also cost his team the game last night. 
2. Marsolo ⬆️9️⃣ (4-1). Another 4 game win streak after a shaky start. A couple wins against bad teams in there but this team is hot right now. Christian Watson is having a breakout WR1 campaign & Bowers coming back has helped this team a lot. I thought this team lacked a true #1 guy but maybe they can be great by committee. 
3. BradleyWeik 🟰 (4-1). 1 bad week in week 2, but otherwise this team has won 3 games in a row and still has one of the highest points for in the league. Omarion Hampton is looking a little like a bust but it doesn’t matter, d smitty has been out as well and still putting up wins. 
4. WoahItsKyle ⬇️3️⃣ (3-2). Don’t get me wrong, I still think this squad is the favorite to win it all. However, a 2 game skid has dropped this team down a couple spots. Still the most points in the league while dealing with some injuries & a bye week. Allen-Henry-Walker should be able to carry the scoring, so look for this ranking to improve. 
5. Rtuebe ⬆️9️⃣ (3-2). Trust the process. Maye, CMC, & JSN are a great trio that should lead this team to the playoffs. Drake Maye got his brain back after becoming a mental patient in the first couple weeks, & George kittle has stepped up to cover Harold fannin jr being a huge bust. 
6. BoricuaBandits ⬆️1️⃣ (3-2). A close loss this week. Jayden Daniels coming back and putting up solid number is a great sign, because otherwise they would have to rely on Malik Willis (ouch). 
7. Juebelacker ⬆️5️⃣ (3-2). This is one of the most hot & cold teams in the league, which is expected when you rely on Burrow, Chase, & Davante Adams. This team will live & die by those players, but the running backs on this team make me want to throw up.
8. Conejos malos ⬆️1️⃣ (3-2). This is the Brock Purdy & Jahmir Gibbs show. End of story. Two great players who will have to continue to carry this squad if they want to make the playoffs.
9. Squallie ⬇️5️⃣ (2-3). A Monday night miracle & a few bad weeks have Squallie with a losing record, but he still has the most points in his division. I had to slide him down but this team is still really good and should make playoffs. Caleb Williams will come back & inject life into the locker room. 
10. Love Hurts ⬇️4️⃣ (3-2). Hanging on by a thread but still picking up wins. I honestly can’t believe this team has been fine & possibly even better without Barkley. In a tough division but playoffs could be in the picture. Maybe Marvin Harrison will play like his dad after being reunited with his college qb.
11. Team Dodz ⬇️3️⃣ (3-2). Winning record but on the lower end of points scored, which just screams fraudulent. Jaylen Warren has been a surprising bright spot but Henderson has disappointed. It’s time for Efton Chism the GOAT to step up for Team Dodz.
12. Team Lorc ⬇️🔟 (2-3). Yikes. Biggest drop in the league. It seems like a fine team value-wise but week 1 was the high point of Team Lorc. Drafting Madison Beer’s fiancé over Patrick mahomes will be questioned for decades. 
13. Merc & friends ⬇️8️⃣ (2-3). Merc had a very good week, but not ready to bump him up just yet after a previous 3 game skid. Looking at the roster though, this team does have good players and could be a sleeper playoff team later in the year. He inserted Penix into his lineup and hopes he’s not gonna bust like Cam Ward.   
14. BastardInTheNorth ⬆️1️⃣ (1-4). Honestly not a horrible team. Starting Barner over Warren is a little suspect so I have my eye on that. I’ll just say I’m happy for this team & that they won a game.
15. Team Carbyy ⬇️1️⃣ (0-5). Rough team. Seriously look at this roster, it’s 80% IR. Starting a kicker on bye suggests maybe this guy has checked out but I’ll give him the benefit of the doubt for another week. Probably assumed his other kicker was injured like the rest of the team. Seeing my goat parker Washington on this team makes me want to cry. Him & McBride are solid pieces though and with achane could be a dark horse for next year. 
16. Throwsa Parks 🟰 (0-5). This is intentional. He’s bad on purpose okay guys? He could’ve won year 1 but decided to pick these guys instead. He’s got us right where he wants us.
